#DomainOfDread #Melonia  


Lead by a Witch who was a factor in the calamity starting named Melonia. 

People say Melonia was the wielder of magic in a time magic was frowned upon and against the will of the Gods. Melonia sought to disrupt these social normals and was ultimately successful. She created many of the spells mages of today can cast and wrote the first scriptures of what each school of magic are. Calling her the strongest mage is an insult to her true potential, Melonia does not seek Godhood on the thought that it would be boring to her. Her true goals are unknown to anyone and she rests at the top of her tower in a Domain of her creation far away from the impact of Gods.



The place Melvain is a city within Melonia where Witches Sorceresses and Mages spend their lives learning magic from the passed down teachings of  Melonia herself. Some speculate that she is dead and the instructors keep it secret, some believe her to be a God but most believe she rests within her tower never reachable and a myth to all.
