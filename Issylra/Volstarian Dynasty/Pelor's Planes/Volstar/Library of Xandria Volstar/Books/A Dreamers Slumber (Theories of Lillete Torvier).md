We hold no answers to their true intentions, we hold no answers as to where they came from but we can find out through the research of Dunamis and the study of its power drifting into the endlessness of the Astral Sea.

Who is the Raven Queen and why does she stalk me when I make new discoveries on this topic and how is she the only God capable of breaching past the Divine Gate and appearing with a true physical image in our world.

Something happened between her and the Raven Queen and Dunamis is the connection it is the key to every door in our universe, the most raw form of magic pulled from the heart of the sleeping Goddess herself.
