#Lore

Location Library of Xandria Volstar Forbidden section session 22. 

In the continent of Wildfort there were many rising arcanists all brilliant in their own right and although they could not rival the knowledge and power of Armount they had their own accomplishments.

The Nation of Cilindrath:

This nation began to rise around the same time as Armount and is the reason it floated in the skies as the Cilindrathies were the first to discover and utilize brimstone. They were able to make ships that once soured through the skies, eventually creating the technology that was sold to Armount allowing to float.

Founded by a woman named Divina Cilindrath this nation prospered during the Age of Arcanum and were experts in the use of a local resource that could only be found within the lorded over vicinity of it. Also being the capital nation it is where the council of Wildfort lived. Divina and 6 others created the continent and all its cities during this period using the airships made from brimstone to fast track all imports and exports within the continent.

In the shadows hiding from Divina a sickness and disease began to grow a cancer to the world known as the Unhileg. This cult although known of for some time took the world by surprise, at first people thought they worshipped Asmodeus but these were falsities placed by their most trusted cult Lord Verazanoth. He planted ideals in the minds of these aspiring arcanists deforming them and corrupting them to become Arch Liches how yearned to ruled the world in the name of their God The Whispered One.
