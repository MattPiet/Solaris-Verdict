#Session_Note
The party Comes In Session 1

So the party after waking up gets summoned by Caesar and is tasked with going to Askain infiltrating enemy lines and killing the front line general named Kuthar reports say he is a goliath berserker with two spell casters by his side constantly protecting him. The party will be teleported directly (as close as possible with a dice roll) to Kuthar and will be given one stone that will teleport them back. Their task is to kill him and his wizard companions, who are unnamed, take all their belongings and any information possible then return in one piece with all they gathered.

Caesar rises and steps forward adorned in obsidian and gold armour, a 9 foot tall grey skinned goliath with three symbols on the front of his heavy armour. 

Caesar “Welcome thiefs I am greatly impressed with your capabilities. In all my years there has never been a group to make it past my watch, you are an interesting bunch with a lot of potential. Your task is to infiltrate enemy lines, slay general Kuthar and his protectors then return with their bodies and any information you gain. Should you choose to accept this task you may keep the items you stole furthermore I will teleport you directly to them and give you a way to come back. Should you choose not to accept then you will spend the rest of your days eating rats in the dungeon I just pulled you out of. Now discuss and choose wisely”
