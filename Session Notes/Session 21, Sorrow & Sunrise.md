[[Sessions.base]]


Last session you all went start in the FeyWild while there you gained power from a lake in Revilyas domain, got the device used for closing the portals (The glorified nuke) and Valrich had a reawakening. However rainbows pixies and colourful skies cannot last forever you all had a mission given to you by the now late Ceasar Volstar, hunt Verazanoth down stop him and strike him down. After the FeyWild you all were transported to Xoras upon your arrival it was very clear that these lands are no longer what they once were. Thousands of undead roamed the outer walls and more lurking within. You all entered through the south entrance and were let in without any resistance as if you were being welcomed in. Once you entered you all saw a barrier above you trapping you in the town, there was no escape. First you stopped at Aralias old home and found a hidden cellar in the basement was the Diary of Divina Cilindrath, Aralias mother, but it sealed shut by a key and the blood of the Lumiere. After grabbing it a Demon appeared and you all slew it, then destroyed a strange portal to hell and the building blew up shortly after you left. Then you made route for the old church to Pelor within the Church there was a Death Knight in Golden armor with the symbol of The Dawn Father engraved on its chest. After failing to sneak past is Cassian made a quick decision to put up a wall of force blocking it up a staircase trapping you all below. Within the basement deshelled famished and barely alive were hidden citizens, hiding away under the protection of the Death Knight summoned by the Pastor who used to run the church, some people who you never heard their names but one definitely stuck out the niece of Sol Xoras. Every person in that basement hates Sol and condemns him for abandoning them they all told him they wish he dies at the top of the tower. Following that you destroyed another portal then made haste to the old town hall turned Askain barracks then now to a house for summoning undead. Within there lied a Death Knight Aspirant trapped to perpetually summon more undead creatures that Verazanoth can control you sealed the portal to the abyss then killed the knight marking the last rift to be closed. Now it was time, time to march to Verazanoth at the top of his tower and take him out with every floor of the tower an new experience of torment and torture awaited each of you killing those whom you love and toying with there remains to then show it was an illusion leaving a doll in their place. At the top of the tower sat the Arch Lich fully awakened Obryn no longer now there is only Verazanoth, after him taunting you all and declaring that Zariel will strike a false hero down and revealing the secrets Aralia has been keeping from all of you a battle took place. The fight for survival, a fight to stop the armies of the underworld, a fight to end the terror of an arch lich, a fight to stop the resurrection of The Whispered One, a fight to save the world of Villatos. Half way through the battle Verazanoth transported the tower to Volstar leaving you all in a free fall before you hit the ground Azest and Alivia arrived telling you that Volstar had fallen and you all need to get there right now. After a teleport spell from them two you arrived in Volstar to the sight of a burning city and a black obsidian tower on the far south side of it on top of the tower rested a portal leading directly to the first layer of hell Avernus. At the foot of the wall stood one man in front of him a ancient red dragon, the lords of each layer of hell and Verazanoth. The skies filled with white snow and fire from the army of thousands of flying devils and demons, the ground covered in snow stepped on by millions of undead. Ceasar Volstar once a king then a hero of the world then the last bastion of hope for all of Villatos killed every single creature on the battle field except for two Zariel was thrown back into the portal and he used your device to close it right before Verazanoth punctured his chest grabbing his heart pulling it free and destroying it. Verazanoth then grabbed two artifacts from Ceasars body and eye and a hand, he pushed the eye into his socket and before he could do anything with the hand an hooded figure shot forward in a flash severing Verazanoths arm taking the hand. This man declared himself as Achillies 2nd Prince of Volstar and revealed to Stu that he is a brother to Ceasar. Right as hope seemed in your favour Verazanoth sought to snuff it out again disappearing for a moment then reappearing clutching Edwin Harrison by the neck and brutally butchering and murdering him in front of all of you, the screams from Edwins suffering soul echoed in the stillness of the night lighting a rage and furry within you all and the fight once again picked up this time the goal do not let him reach Achillies before he can create a barrier across the entire nation. The fight felt hopeless with no sight of victory at all. Given a chance to stop time by Azest you all were given a period of preparation which you used to charge Stu Volstar Storm Lords chosen with as much lightening as possible showering him with spells reaching up to 8th level a beam of reality breaking electrical energy shot forward striking Verazanoth giving enough time for the barrier to form. Verazanoth forced to leave you all he left one last message
