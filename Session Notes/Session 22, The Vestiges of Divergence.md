[[Session 22, The Vestiges of Divergence.canvas]]

On the blood stained snowy battlefield the sun rose again and with it the death of Ceasar Volstar cemented permanently in time. After the battle with Verazanoth you all noticed the entirety of the city's people watching you. They watched Ceasar declare you as those who will become the new sword of Volstar while making this proclamation he imbued the power of his sword Sun Splitter in the Hammer of the All-Father creating a new weapon Solaris Calamity. After this weapon was created Stu opened his eyes and was glowing and crackling with golden radiant electricity now marked by both The Dawn Father and The storm Lord. Achillies then gave a speech telling the cities people that a festival will take place in a few days to celebrate Ceasars life. During that break you all did a series of different things Valrich found out both his mother and Neyis Vice are comatose plagued by an arcane sickness known as Dreamers Eternal Slumber, a sickness caused when a mortal attempts to wield more magic then their body physically allows. Aralia spent time with Azest and Alivia. Cassian asked Achillies questions about both him and Ceasar, requesting to see the artifact that Verazanoth attempted to steal. Achillies denied his request telling him that the artifact would drive him insane and make him kill everything he holds dear. Saggnus walked the city streets in hopes to find more on his ring forged in hellsteel. Timora in an attempt to reach out to her mother was met with an answer to her prayers by The Wild Mother herself Milora who told her that her mother is watching and is very proud of her. But all of you went into the neighbourhood helping the citizens repair their homes and aiding the wounded. Following all of that it was time for Ceasars celebration of life, during which you competed in both a eating and drinking competition, winning in both. Near the end of the festival you Alivia approached you all telling you that it was time to walk into the eyes of the public, three speeches were given 1 by Alivia 1 by Valrich and 1 by Achillies. These speeches rallied the peoples hearts and earned their trust. Following that you had a meeting with the newly formed council of The Volstarian Dynasty. During which you met each member some familiar to you others familiar with you. During which you were told of anceint relics known as Vestiges of Divergence, there are 9 in the world and your party carries 3. These relics are used to destroy the dolls given to you by Verazanoth and are relics of unthinkable power, each relic said to hold the power of 100 men. So we pick up in lights respite all of you deciding to head toward Armount in search of the relic left behind by Avandra The Change Bringer.
