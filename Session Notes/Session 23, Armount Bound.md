[[Session 23, Armount Bound.canvas]]]

A group of misfits and outcasts once came together to rob a kings vault and failed. Out of pity a man named Ceasar made them into a group that became Umbra VI of that original group only two remain Arlia Lumiere a elf with a unknown powerful bloodline. Stu Volstar a half Giant man chosen by the Gods of Battle to fight for the worlds future. On their first job the found one Valrich Torvier chained in a basement, a Drow nobleman with a pact to an Arch Fey. After a member Obryn was possessed by the greatest evil of this era Verazanoth and the three members of Umbra were on the brink of death, three people came to their rescue. Cassian Draemont a Human man with a distrust in the divine and fragmented memories of a lost life. Lucy a female orc once a ship captain now a lost soldier. Sol Xoras an Aasimar man with deep grief and a quest for vengeance. This new party traveled the depths of the residuum mines of Yormindar freeing and allying with a Drow woman named Azest Thyless. In the city of Volstar at that time a woman began to perform in taverns, Timora Fayerine a wood elf with a quest to find her father and make peace with her mothers death. On edge knowing the return of Verazanoth was approaching the party were desperate for a way to trap him and keep him from running like he always has. Saggnus Wildbottom a dwarven man, once a worker in Yormindar now in search of a way to save his father came to the party with a item capable of keeping Verazanoth from fleeing them. Lucy decided to see what she could discover in Vasselhiem while the rest of the group went on a quest to seek out an arch fey then take down Verazanoth. A fight lost and a King Fallen a new title was given to a group of heros who drove off an evil arch lich, Solaris Verdict, Sword of the people dwelling within the Volstarian Dynasty.

Last session Solaris Verdict gathered information from the forbidden library, encountered and questioned a Devil known as Dis and began to ready themselves for a journey to Armount. So we pick up the night before the journey in Lights Respite where Timora Fayerine and Elly prepare for a dinner with Aralia and Azest.
