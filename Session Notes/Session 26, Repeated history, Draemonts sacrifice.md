[[Session 26, Repeated history, Draemonts sacrifice.canvas]]

Last session you all started you're journey in the woods below Storm Crest. Upon you're arrival you met Argdin at the gates of the village who brought you all to the forbidden falls where Stu would go through a trail to inherit his birthright. Failing the trail very close to the end Stu died, with no way of resurrection, no diamond for revivify. Cassian Draemont could not accept the death of his friend, using a ritual once used to revive his father decades ago, Cassian took a chance and exchanged his life for Stu. All that remains on the material plane of Cassian Draemont is a arm made of Dark Silver. Lost and not knowing where to go next Solaris was reminded why they started this journey and who they started it for, Verazanoth. In mourning but a new found vengeful fire lit within them Solaris Verdict marched forward to Stus father and beyond him their main goal. After battling Guldar and killing him you now have many paths ahead of you. Go to the Bio labs and save Stus sister, march to Armount proper or seek out Verazanoth once again outside the barrier near Armount. One fallen comrade, a spell to shatter the gates of reality, a key to a realm beyond and a strengthened Verdict to deliver, we pick up in the snowy landscape standing in front of the corpse of Guldar Stonebreaker.
