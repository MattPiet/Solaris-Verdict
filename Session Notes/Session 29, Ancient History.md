Last session we left off with a decision to make and 3 options before you. After making your way into the proper country you quickly discovered the horrors within. Strahd appeared with a threat and a promise, you went to a prison called Barovia, lost your guide due to disrespect and Thenda was completely taken over by the soul of Ireena Koloyana. You all made way to the Hidden entrance to Bellmont territory and were saved by the current era Belmonts before teleporting to the shop of a woman named Venthra. Then you went to a brewery, discovering new technology on the way and within. Although a lot of drinking happened you also walked out the proud new owners of a pepper box. While testing out the pepper box and using dunemancy Aralia heard a voice in her mind telling her to stop using magic like that with no caution. Later revealing herself to be Venthra Unhileg a woman who many people told you to seek out, Venthra revealed herself as the daughter of The Matron of Ravens and aided you in uncovering secrets nobody else seemed willing to answer. After seeing everything that took place over but a few days Linarian began to fall under the pressure and in his despair The Everlight came to his side. However she did not come to comfort him she came to remined him who he is and what he must do and did not pull her words. In the end you all are left with a choice, seek out Old Lumiere and discover secrets about Aralias past and where to fins Koraki Kastro, disobeying Strahd in the process, find the Sunsword locked withing the Hollowed crypt or go to Castle Raven Loft and bargain for the key to a dungeon containing the Change Bringers staff.
