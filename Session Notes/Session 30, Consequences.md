Last session Started in the shop of the daughter of the Raven Queen and ended in a battle with a knight in dark steel armor. You all made your decision to go to old Lumiere and seek out the mist talisman that will take you to Koraki Kastro. Travelling via Giant Egale you all made it there in record time. However before getting to old Lumiere Valrich made a wonderful home for you all, decorated in heart shaped beds and metallic poles on stages. After a night in a brothel you all made it to Old Lumiere where you were instantly greeted by a Purple Worm who swallowed two of you, eventually you defeated it and entered the home. Within you discovered a old set of earrings and a trapdoor leading to a basement. Below you entered a room with a cauldron and a letter telling you to prove that you are of the Lumiere bloodline you then teleported to a room where you saw multiple statues. One of which shattered revealing a knight that began to battle you in the astral sea. We pick up with all of you near death staring at a man with black hair and pointed ears.

Vax'ildan

"I'm glad you over came your first trial my grandchild, in your next you will see just how deep our Matrons sorrow is. Take your rewards but do not rush into what you are not prepared for. Lastly your mother wants you to know she is proud of the woman you have become, walk with purpose choose your fate the way we all did before you."

After that the party will receive a message from Alivia Divina telling them that they need to get back to the material plane now and that Azest has left the dome to find a way to kill Verazanoth without putting them in danger again.
