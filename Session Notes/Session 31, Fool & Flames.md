[[Session 31, Fool & Flames.canvas]]

Currently most of Solaris Verdict stands in the halls of lights respite after escaping Strahd Von Sarcavich. After passing the first trial of the Raven Queen, acquiring knowledge and a cursed item you took a long rest then were visited by the Lord of this realm who was furious that you did not listen to his caution and warnings. After speaking up Valrich was taken from you and the rest, transported to a forest. After doing battle with and defeating the Umbral Lord Vilaka you all were given a choice, choose one who must make a sacrifice for the others. Timora stepped up taking the mantel, Strahd drank the blood from her neck and tossed her aside. You all tried to wound the immortal but all of your attempts were futile and in the end he let you all walk free. In the meantime First drow noble in all of the continent of Volaria Azest Thelyss could not sit still while her comrades and girlfriend fought alone and she took it upon herself to find a way to take down the Arch Lich Verazanoth. After convincing Venthra and the Belmonts to let you take Venthra with you, you left Trevor Solaris Calamity and made way back to the material world. While Venthra was opening a portal to Villatos Strahd came to taunt you all and keep you from leaving, showing off the newest addition to his Umbral Enforcers a newly awaken Timora Fayerine. In a hurry she pinned him down and held him back buying you enough time for Alivia Divina to come and open the portal for your return. So we pick up there the portal still open behind you standing on the marble stone of Lights Respite.
