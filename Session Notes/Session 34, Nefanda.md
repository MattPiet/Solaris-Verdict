Last session you ended on a beach surrounded by the people of the Menagerie. Aralia took Venthras offer in becoming a wielder of magic. Linarian brought a unfamiliar face to aid you all in your upcoming battle. While that was happening the rest of you were chatting with Issac. That day ended with the death of Duke Aratis lingord. However on his way out he took a childs life with him, after a failed revivify Linarian pleaded with Samira to bring him back. He then rushed to Issacs to get materials needed for a ritual. At this time Stu, Sol, Saggnus and Venthra were interrogating a foe you captured during the fight. She revealed herself to be the avatar of Bhaal Orin The Red, a despicable creature that thrives on the suffering of others. After she vanished Saggnus wanted to know what was happening and who Orin is and answering his call was the Devil Mizora. Mizora offered a deal, forsake Moradin sign his soul to her and he would deal with both Zariel and Strahd no longer, his father would suffer in torment no more. After consideration he accepted the contract and there is no going back. So we pick up on the beach, waves reaching to your boots in the sand and the cheering of a grateful town behind you.
