[[Session 36, Lady of Loss.canvas]]

Last session started with you all starring up at the father of your fallen comrade and ended with you slaying him as he took on a new unsightly form. After both Aralia and Stu were teleported away you took a quick second to catch your breath and try to make a plan. Entering Nefanda you were greeted by a weird green blob thing following that you gave your blood and walked down further into a chamber where you saw Aralia and Stu hanged, bloodied, beaten and in fear for their lives, tortured by Orin the Red. Following that you entered a laboratory where three figures argued and spoke, Drahko Zorloth, Orin the Red and formally known as Obryn now Verazanoth, a vessel no longer. These three were draining the life force of Azest Thyless and in an attempt to both get the dreamers heart from her and open a portal to The Whispered One. Then a portal appeared and a disgusting lich like hand reached from it and Verazanoth handed it his right eye. Gabriel Draemont was revealed and took on a form given to him by The Undying One and you beat him. Leaving this island you found the nearly butchered form of Francis and began to formulate ideas of the past and what to do next.
