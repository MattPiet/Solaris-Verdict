Last session you ended with a letter in your hands standing in a ruined castle and started within Melonia. After the battle you all took a rest and Venthra communicated with her mother, she then reviled it was time for another trial set by the Raven Queen. Following that you spoke with Alura The Cobalt Princess who told you of a relic called Whisper created by the Matron and lost in time. You then traveled into The Void where time was weird and the atmosphere was a void, different places scattered in time and creatures of horrors lurking within. All of you kept your minds together except Stu who succumbed to madness and became a weight slowing the party down. Arriving at Dolor Mortis you all were able to enter except for Stu, when all was thought lost Venthra surged her magic far beyond any point in her life to bring Stu back from near death while that was happening Aralia in an attempt to help used Dunamancy and rendered herself lost to The Void. At that moment a creature appeared attacking you all. Linarian and Saggnus fought off the creature and Saggnus too almost was lost to The Void then Venthra returned to help and saw Aralia lying on the floor, once again she defied the odds and brought another member of Solaris Verdict back from The Void. Aralia and Linarian then saved Saggnus and everyone was out standing on the rocky floor of Dolor Mortis. Venthra was changed and became a true daughter of a God and you all began the second last Trial of the Raven Queen.
