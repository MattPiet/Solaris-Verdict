Last session was a rollercoaster, starting at the bottom of a castle surround by the shadow of the Raven Queen and ending face to face with the consequences of planar travel. After passing a challenge of the mind and understanding her grief you were met face to face with a creature that once took the arm of a friend. However this time it took a life, a beholder turned Saggnus to dust but was defeated in the end by the rage of Solaris. At the top of the citadel you found the key to a book written by Ravina Lumiere telling the story of her life and how she became the Raven Queen, it then told the story of Maelle and more secrets are buried within it. After allowing you to know what she is she gave Aralia a choice, give her fate to the Raven Queen and she will return her friend, with that Saggnus walks the realms once again and you all returned home. Upon your arrival you were plagued with the sight of your maid hanged and the dome destroyed, quickly you went to speak with Achilles who told you that Alivia fought off The Dead Three but is missing and he has no idea where they are now. While being unsure of what to do next you all heard the sickening voice of Verazanoth who declared that in three days he will have the Dreamers Heart and Azest Thyless will be dead. Stuck following his ploy not sure where Lady Thyless is, Solaris what is your Verdict.
