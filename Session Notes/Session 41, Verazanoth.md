[[Sessions.base]]

Last session you all journeyed to Vasselheim where you met up with Lucy once more, visited a place called the Slayers Take, shopped at the home of a Hag and bought a lot of residuum powder from a very interesting sales man. While at the Take you spoke to two people and recruited them to aid in your fight one Kashaw a cleric dawning a holy symbol you dont recognize and Zahra a tiefling sorcerer who was very flirty with all of you but took a liking to Venthra. After a few days of rest and laughter you all teleported to Azests domain of dread known as Veridian. Once there Azest explained to you all her life story and what happened to her wife, how she died and how Azest became consumed by the dread of losing that which she loves. After hearing her story you all saw a tear open inspace and Verazanoth step through it saying a few words before turning your attention to a fracture behind you. Clawing open a rip in space and showing herself after though missing Alivia Divina but not who she once was, a corrupted tortured soul forced to do Verazanoths bidding. Rage and hatred rushing through you for three of you you are once again facing the returning evil Verazanoth however this time he is not alone and neither are you. Underneath the star light marble flooring in a realm born out of despair caused by the creature standing before you, this fight does not dictate the fate of your lives. This fight will that nobody will ever know is the deciding factor to bring about his return, will the Dreamers Heart fall into the hands of the Unhileg, the artifact born to purge their evil or will you stop him here and now.
