Nearly a year ago a group of people marched up the tower of a Lich he was older than any living creature in a hope to stop what he was plotting. Umbra VI battled Verazanoth at the top of the tower of Nazron, failed and it resulted in the death of a Caesar. Following that when morning came a new name was born, Solaris Verdict a group whos mission aligned with that of the Volstarian Dynasty, hero's who swore to avenge the fallen king and bring death to the arch lich. Their first stop was Armount a that once soared through the skies now a collection of ruble where great arcanists who thought their names were forever written in history are forgotten memories. On their way there they made a stop in Storm Crest village killing the father of Stu Volstar and losing the life of Cassian Draemont. Then they made way to the Bio labs in hopes to find The Change Bringers staff. Instead they found Strahd Von Zarcavich the first vampire and an evil as old as time itself, after a confrontation them and a new ally Linarian were forcefully transported to Barovia. While in Barovia Solaris gained an ally Venthra The adopted Daughter of The Raven Queen and they lost Timora to the Lord of Barovia, Strahd Von Zarcavich, The First Vampire. After escaping Barovia Solaris chased after Azest Thyless heading to Wildfort, along the way they stopped in The Menagerie Coast where they liberated the city and found information on Azests where abouts. Solaris and their new crew captain Orlie set sail for The Drowned Anchor, while there they bought a few items lost a gun and got the Pirate King killed. Their next stop Insula Nefanda and The Laboratory of the Whispered One. Waiting for them was the father of the fallen Cassian Draemont who attacked then kidnapped two of you. Within Nefanda they found The Dead Three, Orin The Red, Drahko and Verazanoth, a fight took place with the avatar of The Whispered One. Following Nefanda they made way to Wildfort and then to Melonia where they met the cult which follows The Whispered One. On that day the mayor of Xroas fell and with him hopes of success until Saren Rae, The Everlight to most, The Mistress of The Void to select few chose a new champion to burn this world in her wrath, Linarian Veykar. Traveling for many months through The Void Solaris nearly lost another two member if not for the work of Venthra. Arriving in Dolor Matris Aralia questioned for her mistakes Venthra given a new love from her mother and Solaris debating if their fight is worth the risk, none the less they march on discovering the hidden secrets of Ravin Lumiere and Aralia being given the gift of Immortality and the revival of Saggnus, in exchange she paid with her fate. After being given a path by Azest Solaris traveled to Vasselheim and met with a earlier member of Umbra VI, Lucy. After gaining new allies they marched forward knowing they must confront Verazanoth again with three members who have never even seen his face. Azest gave them her story, her grief, her love and her despair, then he arrived once Obryn now Verazanoth here he was in his full power once more. Alivia reduced to a husk, Azest draining her life force to a point of no return are no excuse to let the flame burn out, against the odds before them Solaris delivered the fury of the sun and Verazanoth was purged from the realms. Then he arrived a whisper as old as secrets, a lich who has survived battles with every figure of power that has lived, The Whispered One, The Undying One, Vecna Unhileg. He killed all of you with Aralia being the only one left standing you all awoke 2 months after that battle with no memory of it and this is where your journey continues, what will you do, who will you save, who will you kill, how will you stop the plot of the greatest evil that no other has succeeded in purging, in this next chapter will you write your names in history or fail like those who walked this path before you.
