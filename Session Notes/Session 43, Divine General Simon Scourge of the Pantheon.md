In the months that passed Solaris Verdict cleaned up many loose ends but they are not finished their fight. After their encounter with Vecna Solaris helped Achilles and Alivia, visited relatives and searched for lost loved ones. After spending some time apart they eventually all reconvened in Xoras where the story of Umbra VI first started and that is where we pick up. Face to face with the dead corpse of Valrich Torvier Solaris needs to choose their next path and fast.
