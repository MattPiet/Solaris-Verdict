[[Sessions.base]]

Millions of years ago before the Calamity lived two best friends, one a kind man who sought to live a life of free will and make a place safe from the upcoming battle of the Gods, the other a man who studied forms of necromancy and unholy arcana reaching a power capable of creating this utopia. The two became something like brothers, and for several decades they built the lands, peace, happiness and a never ending brotherly love built what was later destroyed. During the peak of this creation a woman discovered a way into the realm, the two brothers not seeing any outsiders for decades they welcomed her and showed her their new domain and overtime the woman and the kind hearted man grew close eventually falling in love creating a family with the powerful one to be an uncle. The powerful brother questioned why, why his brother and not him for he his the one who crafted this beautiful realm, he is the one with the power to make all her desires reality, he could not accept this, he must be the one to have her love. Simon who thought that is best friend, brother Strahd only wanted the best for him he never saw his plot coming. Strahd found power through a God currently waging war in the Calamity and he became an immortal in an attempt to please Ireena however she never cared for him and only had love for Simon. Strahd filled with rage he banished Simon from Barovia and turned both his wife and son into vampires and still they would not love him. Simon spent years trying to find a way into Barovia grieving and in destress he discovered a name Killaris a God who has the power to create undead. "If I cannot get my revenge on him then I will kill what gave him his power". Then and there Simon swore an oath, one of revenge and someone, something answered his call and then near the end of the Calamity he got his revenge and Killaris fell to his blade and Simon became a husk giving his soul to Clea in exchange for his revenge. Nobody ever knew the story of Simon Belmont, nobody would ever know, the story of a good friend, father and husband left to suffer for all of eternity because of his best friends jealousy. Today Simon is free his soul no longer trapped in stasis able to finally pass on and rest Solaris Verdict, however his will, his never ending need for revenge has not, Strahd Van Zarcavich must die and the sword of Simon Belmont must deal the final blow.
