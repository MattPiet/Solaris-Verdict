Session 9

Last we left off you all successfully stopped the residuum caravan giving the residuum to Edwin to bring back to Volstar and give it to Caesar. However during the fight although most of you knocked out your enemies Cassian threw a fireball killing 5 guards. Lucy scolded him after the fight then proceeded to kill every witness to the events that transpired there. Also along your trip Aralia was having strange unexplainable visions. Eventually she woke up after a vision one night and felt a pull in a certain direction. Deciding to follow it you all made your way to a cave entrance with a stone staircase and a woman standing by it with the label Tomb of the Fallen Champion. Upon entering the tomb you all were scared to move forward but decided to not trust your instincts and have faith in your friend Aralia. There were two rooms labeled Maelle and Vax'Ildan. You all went inside the room to Maelle where you opened a sarcophagus and Aralia was visted by the Raven Queen herself. After a few minutes and reading a letter written by Maelle to her future generations, Aralia was uncursed of her fiendish form revealing that of a elf with red hair similar to the drawings of Maelle around the room. After that you all went Into the room of the Fallen Champion Vax'ildan. Upon entering it and touching the sarcophagus from a hole in the floor behind you a Behloder begins to rise.

Following the battle Aralia will see a thread coming from her chest to the sarcophagus in the room.

-------------

On the top of the sarcophagus is a dagger and a feather with the letter V beneath it. Inside it is a corpse with incredibly intricate leather armor covered in runes. 

-------------

A black set of leather armor with markings of each element of magic lined with black raven feather around the neck that flows into a cloak flowing down the back. 

-------------

If they just grab the armor a trap will get set off dealing 12d12 necrotic damage requiring a dex save of 18 for it to be halved if this damage brings you to 0 hitpoints you instantly die. This trap can be disarmed with a single cast of dispel magic or an arcana check of 20.

-------------

Once they pick up the armor everything will go dark as if they were in magical darkness and they will all appear right outside the cave entrance.
