#NPC #Volstarian_Dynasty 

Dead

a massively scarred Half-Orc. He does not dress in the velvet coats or feathered hats of high-society pirate captains. Instead, he wears heavy, salt-stained leather, thick iron chains, and boots reinforced with steel. He rules from the highest point in The Crow's Nest, sitting on a throne lashed together from shattered ship wheels, jagged corral, and the rusted cannons of his enemies. When he speaks, it is low and gravelly, forcing the entire room to quiet down just to hear him.

He operates exactly like a ruthless Wild West sheriff in a boomtown. He lets the chaotic partying happen, but the moment violence threatens the stability of his black market, he steps in and handles it personally.

There is no jail on the island. Punishments for breaking the code (stealing from another crew in town, or drawing a blade over a card game) are immediate and public.

His Weapon: The Undertow
