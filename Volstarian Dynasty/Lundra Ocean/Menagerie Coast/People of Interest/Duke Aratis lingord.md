#Volstarian_Dynasty 

a human man who just recently obtained noble power who works in shady business profiting of the poor and increasing the wealth of the rich. He works with pirates to attack smaller vessels and often sends thugs to everyday workers homes to collect illegal taxes. He remains in power because of his connections with The Giver as wells as his abilities in combat.

He is also a devote follower of Bane the Strife Emperor and is part of a bigger group of bane cultists that operate out of the Ruins of Calamity
