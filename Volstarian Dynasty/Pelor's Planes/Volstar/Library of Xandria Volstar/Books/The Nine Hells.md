There are nine layers of hell each of which hold their own disgusting level of torment and torture

Layer 1 Avernus:

Ruled by Zariel, Avernus is currently ground zero for a war that has been on going since the Calamity. Demons and Devils battle each other in whats known as the blood war and the leading general of the Devils armies is Zariel the Fallen Angel. Zariel once a angel, one of Pelors most victorious warriors during the calamity Zariel was a symbol of hope, honor and willpower. She soon became one of Pelor's trusted generals and requested to aid him in vanquishing the Devils in hell, he denied her request telling her that the blood war although brutal is an essential part of the universes existence for those devils are the only things keeping the Demons of the Abyss at bay. Zariel was furious and did not take no for an answer she sought to prove her self to everyone and take matters into her own hands. She took or army the Night Walkers into Avernus and waged war against al fiends insight. A grewsome 3 day battle took place ending with ever soldier in Zariels army dead, defeated and furious at the Dawn Father for forsaking her and not aiding her in her battle she agreed to a deal with Asmodeus becoming his right hand and quickly the most horrifying devil who has ever lived. Leader of The newly resurrected renamed Burning hand, Priestess of agony right hand of Asmodeus, Zariel has spent thousands of years fueling her rage waging a war and waiting for the moment to strike at the Divine Gate breach to the realm of The Dawn Father and kill him in his home.

Layer 2 Dis:

Ruled by Dis Vlakith, Dis is a city of iron and fire. Razor sharp towers over look the crushing tortures heat. The sky is filled with the souls of the dammed and the devils in hell feed of them and trade them as a currency. Humanoid slave labour runs deep in the city and although there are some tieflings and other free humanoid races they are rare and often are the slave traders. Dis is home to the prison Asmodeus uses to lock creatures of all kind away and into eternal unending suffering. Every where you look and walk you can hear or see the screaming deformed souls of innocent and sinners. Within the city of Dis law and order is absolute and any who disobey the laws are subject to becoming lemures. Lemures are fowl souls that are bound to small fleshy golem like creatures that are stripped of their memories, they have humanoid faces and are often seen with their faces frozen in horrifying anguish. They are fodder that are commanded by higher ranking devils and must obey, they do not have free will but their souls are the same as they were before becoming this abomination and are forever reborn and trapped in this eternal torment.

My findings report a lot more then this summary on later pages but just know if you encounter a pit fiend or other creatures of Dis run do not hesitate and do not get caught or you will be stuck suffering the fate of all Lemures.
