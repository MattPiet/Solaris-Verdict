#Lore 

Armount began to rise in place far north east of what is currently Volstar. This city rose as a response to the wave of dunamantic energy created elsewhere in the world. Armount was founded by a man named Iltar Armount, Iltar brought together the brightest minds in Villatos in a hope to harness this strange new power that was washed across the entire world. Created in 950 P.D this city floated in the sky by the use of Brimestone and was kilometers in width, height and length giving it the title of the largest human city on record.

Iltar created a ward known as Dunamis and is where the strange reality warping magic earned its named. Their research discovered many secrets behind this magic but I Achillies could not find exact notes these ruins are far too dangerous.

From what I gather the mages of the Dunamis ward used Residuum and whatever this power source was to conduct research.

Lurking all around Armount are disgusting deformed creatures the reek of fractured and broken Dunamis. From what I gather in the Bio labs the people of this ward conducted sick in human experiments in an attempt to see the affect such potent magic could have on mortals who could not wield magic.

Article 458

"Desarno Finran Wednesday WinterFall the 5th year 960 P.D.

Subject Arabell age 14 magic potency none.

This subject's reaction was most interesting after Residuum implantation in the forearm we charge the ore with Dunamis and the subjects body instantly began to react to it and subject quickly lost their consciousness becoming a mindless creature. These results are typical of most subjects but what follows is most interesting, the subject began seeking out magic and absorbing anything with traces of arcana. The subject absorbed all the arcana within my apprentice turning him into a husk. The more subject 458 absorbs magic the more it grows but if it has not consumed magic for a long amount of time it reverts down to a 8 foot tall creature with long black hair pale grey skin white eyes, 2 foot claws and purple blood flowing through its veins. I Desarno request more subjects lacking in arcana in hope of recreating subject 458. Furthermore I request a replacement apprentice to aid with my experiments."

This leads me to believe that the mages in Armount had real idea what the fuck they were doing and as a result they caught the attention of both the Unhielg and the growing Vasselhiem.

From my lackluster expedition I think Armount was destroyed from the inside. There seem to have been many more who when they found out about these experiments.
